# Process items P-005 and P-006 — the #39 review gate

Opened session 7. These arrived through the normal candidate pipeline as C-S6-002 and C-S6-003, both proposed Tier A. An independent verifier confirmed every underlying fact and **downgraded both on classification**, recommending they leave the F-NNN defect register entirely. The lead adopted that recommendation. They are process items, not findings.

## Why they are not findings

The verifier's argument, adopted in full:

1. This review has drawn the code-versus-process line consistently. Tier A and Tier B mean a code or product defect, and no Tier A finding to date has been about process. Extending the vocabulary for these two would be an ad hoc exception.
2. The remediation paths differ in kind. A code finding is fixed by changing code or tests and re-verified by the suite. These are fixed by writing backfill comments or tightening gate enforcement, neither of which touches `summarizer/`.
3. The precedent already exists and works. P-001 (fabricated status documents) is the same species of integrity finding, kept deliberately apart from F-NNN.

Filing process non-compliance as a Tier A defect would also misdirect remediation, pointing a reader toward a code fix that does not exist.

## P-005 — issue #39 was closed in violation of its own acceptance criterion

**Confirmed.** Severity: major, on the process register's own scale, not the code-defect scale.

Issue #39 adopts an issue-closing review gate. Its own acceptance criterion at line 222 of the traceability matrix reads: "The closing issue comment records the merge and review evidence."

#39 has **zero comments**. PR #47 merged at 2026-09-14T20:51:49Z with `Closes #39` in its body, and the issue auto-closed at 2026-09-14T20:51:51Z — two seconds later, by GitHub keyword, with no human or agent comment. Its own acceptance-criteria checkboxes for rows 220 through 222 remain literally unchecked while the six "Reconciled status" items at rows 214 through 219 are checked.

Verified independently by the lead: `gh issue view 39` returns `comments: 0` and `closedAt: 2026-09-14T20:51:51Z`.

The significance is not the single missing comment. It is that the issue instituting the gate did not itself pass through it, on day one, which is the weakest possible precedent for a process that depends on being applied consistently.

## P-006 — the closing-comment requirement fails across the milestone population

**Confirmed.** Severity: major on the process register's scale.

Of the 17 issues in the "Generalized Recursive Summarizer Rebuild" milestone, 13 have no comments at all. Restricting to the population the gate actually governs — issues closed strictly after PR #47 merged — 6 of 7 have zero comments.

The seventh, #32, received one substantive comment at 2026-09-15T15:34:16Z, **11 hours 31 minutes after** its own close at 04:02:33Z.

**Ruling on that comment, which the S6 reviewer flagged but could not settle:** it does not satisfy the criterion. The requirement is that *the closing comment* record the merge and review evidence. A comment posted half a day later is a disconnected later addition; it eventually supplies the substance but fails the requirement that the evidence accompany the close. The verifier reached this conclusion independently and the lead agrees. Its content also references this review's own remediation work, which makes it a backfill rather than evidence that closing-time practice changed.

## What these items do NOT establish

P-002 asked whether the #39 review gate was applied. These two items answer a narrower question than that, and the distinction matters.

They establish that the gate's **recording mechanism** — rows 220 to 222, the closing comment — was not used, in 6 of 7 governed cases and on the gate's own issue.

They do **not** establish that the underlying adversarial review never happened. Several PR bodies carry real markers of the discipline #39 describes: independently run pytest counts, "reproduced against unfixed main", named regression tests, and in one case an explicit second independent reviewer. Issue #38's closing comment and the late #32 comment are strong positive examples of exactly the practice the gate wants.

So it remains plausible that review activity often occurred and was recorded in the PR rather than the issue. That satisfies the spirit of rows 220 and 221 while failing the letter of row 222. **GitHub artifacts alone cannot distinguish "no review" from "unrecorded review" for the 13 silently closed issues**, and neither item claims to.

## Disposition

Neither enters the F-NNN register nor the GitHub issue-filing batch for code defects. Both belong in whatever process retrospective follows this review. The corrected figures above supersede the ones published in earlier session-7 artifacts and in report.md before this correction.
