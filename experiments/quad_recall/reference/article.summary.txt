Bellweather began planting the first of 2,400 trees on March 18, 2027, in a three-year effort to cool neighborhoods with sparse shade. Mayor Elena Ruiz announced the Canopy Corridors program at Harbor Park, where crews planted twelve coast live oaks along Mason Avenue. The first phase targets four census tracts that ran six degrees hotter than the citywide reading in a 2026 survey, though forester Dev Malik cautioned the survey covered only clear afternoons and is not a complete measure of heat exposure. The council approved $1.8 million on February 27 for planting and two years of maintenance; budget director Nia Cole said a regional grant will cover about half the cost, but the award is not yet signed. Residents may request a street tree beginning April 2, and officials expect the first 700 plantings by December, weather permitting.
